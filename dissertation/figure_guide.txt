WHAT TO INSERT:
  Screenshot of YOUR app's before/after comparison slider.
  
HOW TO CAPTURE:

  3. Upscale it with Real-ESRGAN Pro at 4x
  4. On the result page, position the comparison slider in the CENTER

  - Photo with text/signage (letters get blurry in LR)
  - Landscape with trees/foliage (leaf detail disappears in LR)
